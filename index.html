AutoVillage

Build your village and help it survive!

AutoVillage is a pixel-art 2D game where the fate of a small medieval village depends on your decisions.

Every 15 seconds you get two random actions. Choose what happens next: a new building appears or an unexpected event strikes. Some choices help the village grow, others bring danger.

Watch your villagers, protect the settlement from disasters and try to keep the village alive as long as possible.

How to play: pick one of the two buttons at the bottom of the screen. If you don't choose within 15 seconds, the game picks one at random. Hold a button for about half a second to read what it does — the game pauses while the description is open.

Drag villagers and enemies: wounded units to the Hospital, Cyclopes to the Jail, frightened villagers into houses.
